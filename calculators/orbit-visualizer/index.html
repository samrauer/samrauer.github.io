a — Semi-major axisⓘ Half the long axis of the ellipse. Controls overall orbit size — larger values mean higher altitude and a longer period. 2.50 R⊕
e — Eccentricityⓘ How elongated the ellipse is. 0 = circular; approaching 1 = highly elliptical with a very close periapsis and a far apoapsis. 0.20
i — Inclinationⓘ Tilt of the orbital plane relative to the equator. 0° = equatorial, 90° = polar, >90° = retrograde (orbiting opposite Earth's spin). 45°
Ω — RAANⓘ Right Ascension of the Ascending Node. Rotates the orbital plane around Earth's polar axis, setting where the orbit crosses the equator heading north. 0°
ω — Arg. of Perigeeⓘ Rotates the ellipse within its plane. Sets which direction periapsis points — without changing the orbit's shape or tilt. 0°
ν — True Anomalyⓘ The satellite's current position along the orbit, measured as an angle from periapsis. Does not affect orbital shape — only where the satellite is right now. 0°
