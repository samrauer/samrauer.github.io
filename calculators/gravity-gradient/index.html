How it's computed

Torque on a rigid body: T = 3*(mu/R^3) * u x (J u), where u is the nadir unit vector (body toward the central body's center) and R the distance between their centers.

For each axis pair (i, j), the torque about the third axis is proportional to (Ji - Jj):

  • Max torque (nadir offset 45 deg): (3/2)(mu/R^3) |Ji - Jj|
  • Small-angle gradient (stiffness at 0 deg): 3(mu/R^3) |Ji - Jj| * (pi/180) per degree
